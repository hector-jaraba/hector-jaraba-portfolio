# This Post is From the Future! 🚀

This post demonstrates the **scheduled publishing** feature. It has a publish date set to December 31st, 2025 at 10:00 AM.

## How Scheduled Publishing Works

<Callout type="info" title="Automatic Publishing">
  Posts are automatically published when their `publishDate` arrives. No manual intervention needed!
</Callout>

### Behavior

**In Development Mode:**
- ✅ Scheduled posts are **visible** with a blue "SCHEDULED" badge
- ✅ You can preview and edit them before they go live
- ✅ Warning banner shows the scheduled publish date/time

**In Production:**
- 🔒 Scheduled posts are **hidden** until their publish date
- ✅ Automatically become visible when the date/time arrives
- ✅ No build required - it's all handled at request time

## Setting a Scheduled Post

Just set a future `publishDate` in your frontmatter:

\`\`\`yaml
---
title: 'My Future Post'
publishDate: 2025-12-31T10:00:00  # ISO 8601 format with time
# OR
publishDate: 2025-12-31  # Just the date (defaults to midnight)
---
\`\`\`

<Callout type="success" title="Time Zone Support">
  The `publishDate` supports full ISO 8601 format including timezone information. If no timezone is specified, it uses the server's local time.
</Callout>

## Use Cases

1. **Content Planning** - Write posts ahead of time
2. **Coordinated Launches** - Schedule posts for specific dates
3. **Time Zone Targeting** - Publish at optimal times
4. **Editorial Calendar** - Plan content weeks/months in advance

<Callout type="warning" title="Important Note">
  Remember that this post will automatically become visible on December 31st, 2025 at 10:00 AM. Make sure it's ready before that time!
</Callout>

This feature works seamlessly with drafts - you can have a post that's both scheduled AND in draft mode for maximum flexibility during the writing process.
